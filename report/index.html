1. What was checked

I read all 26 figures of the RR-300 catalog (drawing page and parts table), the bearing and fastener tables and the part-number index from the scans. That gave 253 rows. Figure 2 (the 16-pin coupling variant) is a modification and was left out, which leaves 239 rows and 647 pieces for the base unit. The earlier model had 73 objects. The parts tables list 233 washers, 97 nuts, 74 bolts, 45 rings, 35 springs, 20 liners, 19 discs and 13 bearings (alternative fastener sets are counted in both versions), and almost none of them existed.

Five builders each took a group of figures, compared their parts with the drawings and the manual, fixed sizes, and drew what was missing. A sixth reader extracted every printed dimension from the manual and compared it with the model.

3. Practices taken from the reference kit

  • Source registry. One row per catalog position: part, figure, position, quantity in the catalog and in the model, viewer node, source scans, geometry status. File: catalog/source_registry.csv.
  • Manifest. Nodes, catalog figure, explode offsets and windows: catalog/rr300_manifest.json.
  • Stable names. Every object is called F<figure>P<position>_name, so a click on any bolt shows its catalog row: figure, position, designation, quantity, mass, material. Extras that are not in the catalog start with X.
  • Honest status per part. Drawing-verified (10 rows), catalog dimensions (58), approximate (153), not drawn (18, each with a reason). Approximate parts are never presented as measured.
  • Mesh QA. blender/qa_glb.py checks every GLB for NaN vertices, open edges and non-manifold edges. All new parts pass; the tolerated exceptions are listed in section 6.
  • Linked duplicates. Repeated fasteners share one mesh, so 738 objects fit into 11 MB.
  • Closed solids, bevelled edges, applied transforms for every new part, and clash probes between neighbouring groups.
  • Section view and contact shading. A stencil-capped cutaway (three planes, one slider) and a GTAO ambient-occlusion pass, both switchable.

4. Dimension audit

The manual has no dimension lines. Printed sizes exist only for the overall 781 x 650 x 799 mm, the masses, the 19 mm foot holes and a 46 mm water pipe. Everything else was scaled from the drawings after calibrating on the 238 mm centre distance. The calibration reproduces the three printed overall values to within 1 percent, so the scaled values are good to about 3 to 5 mm.

5. Result

Open rr300-3d/index.html. Click any part to see its catalog row. The button "Cut" cycles three section planes, and the table "Composition by catalog" lists every position with catalog count, model count and confidence.

6. What is still approximate or open

  • Not drawn: nut 329-21 and fitting 525-20-117 (figure 25), and 2 of the 12 sealing rings 18x24 at the sensor bar joint, which has no gap for them.
  • 153 of 239 rows are approximate: the catalog prints no dimensions or tooth counts for them. Sizes were set from standard geometry, part photos, drawing proportions and the catalog masses. Tooth counts of the pump gears (Z77, Z42, Z25) and the clutch hubs are estimates.
  • Solid shafts are heavier than the catalog (oil bores are not modelled): 16.7 against 14.9 kg and 36.1 against 27.5 kg.
  • Bell: the catalog base flange is 650 mm, the model has your photo variants. Overall height and width therefore differ from 799 and 650 mm.
  • Cover 74 (cooler end) was enlarged from the scaled 122 mm to 138 mm to close the 135 mm bore. Its edge overlaps a nut and a flange by 3.5 to 4 mm.
  • Sections: a cap needs a closed mesh. The cover skin is open (2448 open edges), and housing_main (15 weld edges) and a few other meshes have non-manifold welds, so a few cut faces show artefacts.
  • 22 plain studs and four cover studs are extra objects that have no catalog row. The mounting kit is shown only in the exploded view and the driving half-coupling lies flat at the far end of the kit row.
  • Fasteners move with their assembly in the exploded view. They do not yet lift off in their own earlier stage.
  • Filter bowl wall is 8.5 mm at the rear and the idler recess leaves 4 mm of wall at one spot.
  • The page is 11 MB and loads Three.js from jsDelivr, so it needs internet. Draco compression and an offline copy are possible follow-ups.
